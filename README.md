# 🚀 Featured GRC Projects

## 1️⃣ Enterprise Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

**Objective:** Perform an enterprise-level cybersecurity risk assessment for a simulated organization.

### Activities

- Identify critical information assets
- Identify threats and vulnerabilities
- Develop cybersecurity risk scenarios
- Evaluate likelihood and business impact
- Calculate inherent risk
- Assess existing security controls
- Determine residual risk
- Develop risk treatment recommendations
- Assign risk ownership
- Prioritize remediation activities

### Deliverables

- 📊 Enterprise Risk Register
- 📄 Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Report
- 📊 Risk Treatment Plan
- 📈 Executive Risk Summary

**Skills:** Risk Assessment · Risk Register · CIA Triad · NIST CSF · Control Assessment · Risk Treatment

**Status:** 🚧 In Development
